An AI Readiness Assessment is a structured review that identifies where artificial intelligence can save time, reduce repetitive work, or improve customer response without adding unnecessary operational risk. Sarran AI Solutions begins with one 45-minute discovery call covering the tasks, handoffs, delays, and systems that shape a normal working week.

Each opportunity is evaluated across four factors: potential time saved, implementation effort, recurring tool cost, and the level of human oversight required. The resulting written plan ranks 3 to 7 opportunities, explains the reasoning behind their order, verifies expected software costs, and identifies a practical four-day starting plan.

Recommendations may include voice agents, workflow automation, AI-assisted intake, reporting, follow-up, or web-based qualification. The client owns the plan and can implement it independently or ask Sarran AI Solutions to build selected systems. The full assessment is designed to take about one week while requiring very little additional client time.

AI Voice Agents

Answer common questions, qualify callers, and capture or schedule appointments when your team is busy or closed.

  • 24/7 call coverage
  • Qualification and scheduling
  • Reminders and approved follow-up
  • Human handoff when needed
Explore Always On

An intelligent AI voice agent is a phone-based system designed to handle approved conversations consistently, including when a team is busy or the business is closed. The implementation begins by defining the calls the agent may answer, the information it may collect, the questions it must ask, and the situations that require transfer or human follow-up.

The agent can qualify inbound callers, answer approved questions, schedule appointments in an existing calendar, and run permitted outbound reminders or follow-up calls. Each conversation can produce a transcript and call-level record so the business can review what happened, identify exceptions, and refine the workflow.

Production controls are designed before launch. Integrations receive only the access they need, sensitive actions use explicit confirmation, and uncertain or high-impact situations are escalated to a person. The result is extended call coverage without asking the system to make decisions outside its defined role.

Explore Front Door

An AI-integrated website combines a fast, accessible marketing site with focused automation that helps a visitor reach the right answer or next step. The work starts with the conversion path: what the visitor needs to understand, which questions indicate fit, and where a request should be routed.

AI is added only where it removes friction. Appropriate uses can include answering questions from approved business content, collecting qualification details, routing an inquiry to the correct service, or preparing structured information for a booking request. The website remains usable when an AI feature is unavailable, and important actions stay behind deterministic application rules rather than model judgment.

The underlying build prioritizes responsive layout, semantic HTML, accessibility, and Core Web Vitals. Technical SEO is designed in from the start through crawlable content, descriptive titles and metadata, canonical URLs, structured data, intentional internal linking, sitemap and robots directives, and answer-ready page structure for traditional and AI-assisted search. Content and conversion controls are designed so the business can update routine information without depending on a developer for every change. These foundations improve discoverability and indexing without making unsupported ranking guarantees.

Workflow Automation

Connect routine steps so intake, data entry, reporting, reminders, and follow-up happen consistently without manual copying.

  • Intake and request routing
  • Data movement between tools
  • Reporting and reminders
  • Exception handling for your team
Explore Back Office

Workflow and task automation connects repeatable business steps so information can move, calculations can run, and routine follow-up can happen without manual copying between systems. A workflow may start on a schedule or from an event such as a submitted form, a changed record, an approaching due date, or a completed appointment.

The design documents the trigger, required inputs, business rules, permitted system access, expected output, and exception path before implementation. Suitable workflows can support intake, data entry, reporting, invoicing preparation, reminders, status updates, and internal routing. Existing tools are reused where practical so the automation does not create unnecessary software overhead.

Automation handles only the predictable path. Missing information, validation failures, unexpected values, and sensitive decisions are surfaced to a person with enough context to act. Logs and run-level reporting make it possible to see what completed, what failed, and where the process needs adjustment.

Why it holds up

A demo proves it can work. Production proves it keeps working.

Most AI pilots stall on the same four things: tools called without boundaries, high-impact actions with no approval gate, evaluation that only grades the final answer, and cost that looks fine in a demo and breaks at volume. We design for all four before writing anything.

Talk through your setup

  • A person confirms anything that matters

    Payments, refunds, cancellations, data changes — all gated behind explicit confirmation and deterministic validation. Never a model's inferred intent alone.

  • Every tool is scoped and logged

    Least-privilege access on each integration. Your application code stays the source of truth for business rules — the agent never is.

  • Only the data the step needs

    Personal data is masked, retention is policy-driven, and untrusted content is kept isolated from system instructions.

  • Cost designed in, not discovered

    Cheap models for simple steps, stronger ones only where they change the outcome. Priced at production volume from day one.
