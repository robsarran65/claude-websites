How that experience shows up in this work

Most AI pilots stall on the same four things: tools called without boundaries, high-impact actions with no approval gate, evaluation that only grades the final answer, and cost that looks fine in a demo and breaks at volume. Sarran AI Solutions designs for all four before writing anything — the same production discipline behind the track record above.

  • A person confirms anything that matters — payments, refunds, cancellations, data changes
  • Every tool is scoped to least-privilege access and logged
  • Only the data a given step needs is used, with policy-driven retention
  • Cost is designed in at production volume, not discovered after launch
